## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This story is about how two kind, loving hearts can bring goodness to the entire world. It is about true love being boundless. It tells of a sincere and gentle love that soars with us beyond all dimensions, beyond the mysteries of the [Beskonechnoe leto](/v3126) universe, and beyond the strangeness of this and other worlds.

It is a story about how real love is the strongest force on Earth. It shows that without any special powers, and without being a genius, even the most ordinary person can be truly loved, truly loving, and truly kind.
