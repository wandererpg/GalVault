## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Izumi Tomohiro was appointed student council president by drawing lots. At first, he carries out his duties adequately and without risk, but before he knows it, he begins to feel a commonplace sense of commitment. He becomes so active that he even goes out on his days off with the other members.

One day, Tomohiro is forced to participate in a doujinshi sale as a staff member of a kojin circle. After selling the books, he leaves his seat to buy a drink. On the way, he wanders into the original creation space on a whim, and spots someone who shouldn't be there...

This is the story of a girl who is not the main heroine, and who strayed out ("hamidashi") from the main story.

[Translated from the [official website](https://madosoft.net/rerecall/story)]
