## 简介（VNDB 原文）

If you were insulted, told you were an ugly clown, and you should go join the circus, you wouldn't actually do it. Nobody would, right?

Wrong.

After being called an ugly clown by Hiyoko Saionji, Komaeda takes it seriously and goes to the local circus and becomes a clown. Being taught by professional clown Kokichi Ouma, Komaeda will soon be the best clown to exist.
