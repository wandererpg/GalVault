## 简介（VNDB 原文）

As she started getting used to the Shinsengumi, Chizuru began to have odd dreams.

Hijikata, Saitou, Okita, Harada; they've all been proclaiming themselves to be her husbands.
Being a demon, there's no way she could live happily as a human.
Just as she started forgetting about those dreams, they started coming back to her in real life...

These are the many happy futures she could share with her beloved husbands.
