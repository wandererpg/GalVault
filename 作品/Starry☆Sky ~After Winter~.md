## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Starry Sky After Winter continues from the events of the previous game, with Tsukiko studying in Seigetsu Academy for the third year. It follows the continuations of the routes from Starry Sky in Winter and there is a new route for Kagurazaka Shiki.
