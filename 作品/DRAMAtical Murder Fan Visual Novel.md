## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Based on Nitro+CHiRAL's BL visual novel DRAMAtical Murder. The seven main characters, Seragaki Aoba (and his allmate, Ren), Koujaku, Noiz, Clear, Mink, Virus, and Trip are featured in this little fan visual novel, all in their super-deformed or chibi forms drawn by ppppantsu.
