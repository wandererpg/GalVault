## 简介（VNDB 原文）

<!-- AUTO:BEGIN synopsis -->
A large-scale expansion of the main Sengoku Rance story, approved by AliceSoft, expanding the JAPAN unification storyline and allows further exploration to the continental map and play-trough of Part 2.

The events in this mod progresses with characters who are not supposed to be in the game regardless of the routes taken in the main story. This also applies to generals who have disappeared due to death or dismissal.

Important changes and additions:
-The "Combination System", which allows generals to join forces and fight together.
-The "Blitzkrieg Warfare System", which enables blitzkrieg invasions of enemy countries.
-No use of action power for national power development in the second part.
-Changes to the labyrinth system in the second part.

-Changed game resolution from 4:3 to 16:9.
-Batch whole attack, increased battle speed.
-Replenishment of generals' troops by SHIFT+click.

[Based on the [official website](https://hannylaboratory.blogspot.com/2023/11/blog-post_30.html) and [Twitter](https://twitter.com/ec_zer)]
