## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Sophomore student of Suzaku high school, Kirigaya Haruto stayed at a small country inn along with his childhood friend. Although he was worried about the monetary support from his parents that stopped and the accumulating rent, he lived a laid back life beneath the spring warmth.

While wondering one night, Haruto meets a beautiful girl called Yoshino-Hime. Since that day, something changed within him. Preparing for the biggest festival of the year in 2 weeks, the quiet rural town started to become active...
