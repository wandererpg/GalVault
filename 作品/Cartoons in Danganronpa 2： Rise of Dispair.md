## 简介（VNDB 原文）

An unofficial fan-made sequel fangan based and inspired by the YouTube series ‘Cartoons in Danganronpa’ ThatCheesyB.

Play the role of Huey Freeman, a calm, irritated kid with a sense of justice and equal rights, rejected as an Ultimate for Professor Membrane’s Hope’s Peak Academy, only accepted after winning the ‘Ultimate Lucky Lottery’ to become the Ultimate Lucky Student.

Things were fine until he was knocked out and woken up in an underground bunker, along with 15 other students, including his brother.

What mysteries lie within this bunker? Who is responsible and for what reason? Who will rise? And who will fall?
