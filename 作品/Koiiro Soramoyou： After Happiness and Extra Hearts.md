## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This is the fandisk to Studio Ryokucha’s 2010 title Koiiro Sora Moyou, with after stories for each of the 5 heroines, as well as new stories for the 4 subheroines (Yuuki, Aiko, Kiyomi and Machiko). Returning to peaceful (?) days, Seigo helps the new student council out with the culture festival preparations. Of course, there’s also now more time for sweet love.

[From [Hau~ Omochikaeri!](https://omochikaeri.wordpress.com/2011/10/01/october-2011-eroge-releases/)]
