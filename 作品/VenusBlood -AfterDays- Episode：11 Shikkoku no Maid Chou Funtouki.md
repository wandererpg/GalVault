## 简介（VNDB 原文）

After the battle with the Dark God Overlord Vergius, Anora decided to stay with Sylvia who has become Empress of the Empire, and helped her solidify her reign with timely advice and help.

However, as she spent those busy days assisting Sylvia, her thoughts kept drifting towards her other half, her soulmate, Leonhardt. With the world now at peace, Anora dared to dream of a future together with him where they would marry and settle down.

Taking such a drastic step caused her no small amount of trepidation, for she had no homemaking skills...

After hearing of Anora's troubles, Sylvia suggested she learn on the job. And so begins Anora's tenure as the 'Jet-Black Head Maid' of the castle, as part of her effort to learn homemaking skills. However, it would seem that things may not proceed as smoothly as she would have liked...?
