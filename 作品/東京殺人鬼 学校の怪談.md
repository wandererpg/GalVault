## 简介（VNDB 原文）

<!-- AUTO:BEGIN synopsis -->
Amaterasu has existed since ancient times as the supreme deity of the country of Japan, while Aramatsuri has existed as a symbol of the evil forces.

Good and evil, happiness and unhappiness, life and death—all of these, originating from Amaterasu and Aramatsuri, are inherent within human beings.

Aramatsuri sometimes materializes, and at other times it causes trouble in the world by negatively affecting humans.

The protagonist, Yuzuru Kurotori, is a civil servant working for the Tokyo Metropolitan Government. He belongs to the Aramatsuri Countermeasures Sector on the 7th Floor of the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building.

Only those who can see and stand toe to toe with Aramatsuri belong to this department, and while there are various selection methods, the final criterion is whether they can see the giant flower on top of the clock tower in Yoyogi.

It's been three years since Kurotori joined the organization.

Even in modern Japan, the Aramatsuri wanders the streets of Tokyo…
