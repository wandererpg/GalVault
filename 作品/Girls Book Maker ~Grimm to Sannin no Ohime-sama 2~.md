## 简介（VNDB 原文）

"I want to be a newlywed!"

Eito, who continues to act as the acting prince, asks for help for the character "Ibara", who is said to wake up only when called by the "prince". Naturally Eito, who is not a real prince, is unable to wake her up, but the next day he finds Ibara napping in the city street.

He wakes her up and asks her what he should do as the acting prince. And she says to him, "I want to live a newlywed life!"

[Edited from [Official Website](http://yumemiru.nexton-net.jp/gbm-grimm/episode02/)]
