## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Head on up to Camp Ash with Chip Bronson during the Summer of 1974 in this Horror-Comedy Visual Novel! He's joined by his girlfriend, Janice Adler, and his best friend, Dale Cooper. The trio set off to work at a University-Sponsored Summer Camp. Other locals from their hometown of Echo end up working at the yearly spot... as well as a foreigner with a familiar fur pattern. Not everything's as it seems though as the weight of a dark secret follows our otter into the mountains. Help decide his fate and the fate of his coworkers in this choice-based visual novel featuring six different routes, each with three unique endings!

[From [Itch.io](https://hiredzombie.itch.io/camp-ash-vn)]
