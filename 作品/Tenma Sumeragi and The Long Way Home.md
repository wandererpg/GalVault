## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Happy Birthday Tenma 2022!
Inspired by an A3 event where rankers on the front page wrote google map coordinates to point to various places saying that Tenma was lost.

That then inspired a scavenger hunt I made last year to play in an a3 discord server which involved answering trivia in order to move from location to location and find where Tenma has lost himself to.

AND THUS, THAT BRINGS US HERE.

A Visual Novel game that takes the same concept of solving trivia to go to new locations coupled with chatting with A3 characters in different AUs and helping them out.


Yes, the entire cast of A3 is here.

Yes, they are all part of some... strange AU.

Yes, they all don't know who Tenma is.

[From [Itch.io](https://no-xiruo.itch.io/tenma-sumeragi-and-the-long-way-home)]
