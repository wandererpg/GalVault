## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Long time ago, the Four Aegises created countless worlds which exist in parallel universes. The mysterious Day of Full Veerima stretches the barriers of this multiverse thin, so lots of miracles are about to happen. Princess Beexen concealed a pact with the forces of primordial darkness in a desperate attempt to restore justice and claim her father’s throne which had to be rightfully hers. However, the price of the crown was too high. As a Herald of the First primordial phantom of darkness Tai-Ahnara, she was ordered to lead their armies to conquer the shattered and devastated world of Dreamstead and spread the raging war even beyond its boundaries.
