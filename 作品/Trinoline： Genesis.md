## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Fandisc for Trinoline. Contains stories for all the characters (including sub-characters Hanako and Ayaka). [Soreyori no Prologue](/v15637) character poll winner Himeno Touwa's mini story is also included.

In a questionnary, minori asked for people to put what they want in. The [questionnaires](http://www.minori.ph/lineup/trinoline/_genesis_.html) was open until May 22nd.
