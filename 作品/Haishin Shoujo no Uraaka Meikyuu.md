## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Trapped in a labyrinth of secret accounts...
To escape alive, the method is simple.
Find the account owner, and kill her.

Rag Drops, a girl band centered around their lead singer, "Neon", gains immense popularity on SNS. However, one day, the news breaks out of Neon having【passed away】. As uproar spreads across the internet, an anonymous account by the name of 'Poison Apple' points the finger towards Rag Drops --

"Neon died, all because of you."

Caught in the midst of unprecedented controversy, who was it that made that accusation—? Surrounded by a whirlwind of suspicion, suddenly, the phone screen lights up... The girls are transported to the secret world of accounts, 'Ura-U', embodying the darkness of the internet.

[Translated from [the Official Website](https://hanoura-maze.com/)]
