## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The story follows a young man struggling with dissociative identity disorder and various addictions. His most painful dependency is on his girlfriend, and he desperately tries to keep their relationship from falling apart. Whenever he faces serious problems, control is often taken over by his other personality, more impulsive and radical, one that tends to solve issues in drastic and often irrational ways. These sudden transformations lead to dangerous and unpredictable situations. Amid his inner battles and unstable reality, the only person trying to keep him from total collapse is his friend Stas, who desperately attempts to guide him toward a path where he can survive and, perhaps, find himself.
