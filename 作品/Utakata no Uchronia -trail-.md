## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This fandisc to Utakata no Uchronia includes "AFTER STORY" and "ANOTHER STORY" which depict the aftermath of the original game, as well as "IF STORY" presenting new routes for Hiiragi and Tsuwabuki. You can access any of these stories from the game's start.
