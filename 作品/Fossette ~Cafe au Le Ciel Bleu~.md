## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This game is a fandisk of Kono Aozora ni Yakusoku o, Parfait and Chocolat...

What are the contents?

- SIDE TSUGUMI SEVEN (Kono Aozora ni Yakusoku o Chapter)
a) Tatakau Osananajimi
b) Sei to Shizu no Fungai
c) Ikenai! Sae-chan-sensei
d) Watashi no Kakera
e) Watashi ni mo Tsubasa o Kudasai
f) Shishi Shinchuu no Mushi yori Hara no Mushi
g) Kono Aozora ni Utagoe o
h) Hidamari no Virgin Road


- SIDE FAMILLE (Parfait ~Chocolat Second Brew~ Chapter)
a) Katrea Kinenbi
b) Mayoeru Hitsuji to Chimimouryou
c) Kasurin no Ren'aijuku FINAL
d) Rikako Shou
e) Datsuryoku Katrea Kinenbi

- QUIZ MINIGAME

- MEMORIAL MODE
a) Event Mode Memorial (Replay H-scenes from the three game but you need to install them first to play)
b) Chara Memorial (View sprites of the characters from the three said games)

[Translated from [Getchu by Adellus](http://www.getchu.com/soft.phtml?id=334465)]
