## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Months have flown by since the protagonist moved to this town and crossed paths with these lovely girls... The seasons have shifted, and now autumn is in full swing.

Autumn—students recognize it as the harbinger of a grand event - the school cultural festival! Our protagonist is knee-deep in a frenzy of preparations for this momentous event.

Today, tomorrow, and the day after, it's all about getting ready for the cultural festival after school!
Prepare, prepare, prepare!
But no matter how important the preps are, he knows spending time with his girlfriend is just as crucial.
Yet, even with this knowledge...

As the protagonist's personal time dwindles, the heroines' frustrations mount!
They put on a brave face, but their pent-up desires are nearing a boiling point.
Some can't hold it in any longer and lash out at the protagonist!
Still, the cultural festival is almost here!
Once it starts, they'll have no choice but to enjoy it together!

*Takoyaki*, crepes, haunted houses... and even a myriad of peculiar stalls and events!
How will our protagonist navigate the cultural festival with the heroine by his side...? The ball is entirely in his court!
