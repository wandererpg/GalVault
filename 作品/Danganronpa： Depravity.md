## 简介（VNDB 原文）

16 Ultimate Students wake up on an almost lifeless cruise ship with seemingly little to no land in sight. Adelaide Deirdre, Ultimate Political Speaker, searches the ship for any other people or threats that lie within the ship. She runs into Axel Jones, Ultimate Surfer. After interacting with the other 14 Ultimates, a strange message appears on Adelaide's monopod. Which instructed her to head to a so-called "meeting room". To which she finds out that there's more to this cruise. She finds out that she and the other 15 Ultimates are here for one purpose and one purpose only. To kill or be killed...
