## 简介（VNDB 原文）

You're following the story of Lu, a 21-year-old sweetheart who got accepted into the prestigious Talent Program as the Ultimate Relationship Counselor <3

She finds herself at the Monoco Resort and Casino, a fancy retreat that was booked by the INCREDIBLY generous Talent Program for a Super Special Program; all for a special group of 17 students! How lucky are they!

The Super Special Program is meant to be the most fun and educational trip of the students' lives! One that has an indefinite end date. Whoops! More time to learn, live, and love alongside your brand new friends!

Buuuut, if a student happens to want to graduate and leave, they’ll have to prove themself through a graduation project; kill one of your classmates and get away with it. We’ll see if anyone can actually pull it off, or maybe they’ll all live in harmony in a fancy resort for the rest of their lives. And that doesn’t sound so bad! Does it?
