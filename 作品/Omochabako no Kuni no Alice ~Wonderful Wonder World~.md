## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This is a fandisc to the ~Wonderful Wonder World~ series of games.

The game is split into two parts:
*  A part of the story is set in wonderland where Alice is put in a position that she leaves the area where she was staying
* A parody of one of Quin Rose's other games where the story is set in a magic school
