## 简介（VNDB 原文）

You can become the homeroom teacher of "Class 3-B" from the [popular TV drama](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/3年B組金八先生)!

You take care of 21 students from Class B in the 3rd year (including transfer students). The game progresses episode by episode like a television show, and resolve the worries and troubles of the students that occur each episode.

And the story takes an unexpected turn...

Will you be able to get your students to graduate safely?

[Translated from [official website](https://www.spike-chunsoft.co.jp/games/3b/info.html)]
