## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Fate is a fact. Those who argue otherwise are mere fools. History has proven this to us. Yet, despite everything, humanity strives to fight against this fact. You are one of those representatives of humanity known as Okabe Rintarou. Once, you believed that it was possible to plunge the world into chaos and overthrow the Organization, even without knowing what that truly meant. But now, having lost your last hope, what will you do? Perhaps there’s no need to change anything? After all, everything is perfect right now. You stand in the middle of the room, surrounded by friends, and isn't it time for the mad scientist to awaken?! May the Gates of Destiny open to all who desire them!

F;GE: A visual novel that incorporates elements from Fate/Stay Night while using the SciADV universe.
