## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Doujin game inspired by ONE ~Kagayaku Kisetsu e~. Contains three separate stories with three different protagonists:

"Stay.": [Nagamori Mizuka](/c97359) tries to cope with Kouhei's sudden disappearance.
"Before it turns into a memory": [Kawana Misaki](/c97358) tries to find out what happened to [Orihara Kouhei](/c97354), with the help of [Kouzuki Mio](/c97357) and [Miyama Yukimi](/c97360).
"Sweet": Orihara Kouhei and [Satomura Akane](/c97355) enjoy their daily life as boyfriend and girlfriend.
