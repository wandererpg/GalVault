## 简介（VNDB 原文）

<!-- AUTO:BEGIN synopsis -->
Your house is your castle – a well-known proverb. But what if you’re forced to share it with someone who’s not quite all there? And can’t resist the temptation to steal something right under your nose?

Yoru was a common operative agent, when one day he was knocked over by a thief quite well-known in the criminal circles. And of course he never expected that person to become his partner and, moreover, to settle in his house. And it was impossible to predict what results this meeting would lead to and how closely their fates would intertwine.

They both are haunted by the bleak shadow of the past; the events that happened long before their birth lead to disastrous events in the present.
Nobody remembers now what was once called truth; it’s hidden deep in the shadows of centuries and consigned to oblivion… But how can you be sure that you need to know this truth? What if, because of it, your life one day will scatter into pieces and you’ll never be able to put it back together?

Everyone here has their own philosophy and course of life: helpless victim or bloody murderer, lonely hermit or soul of the party… Whom would you like to be? Are you ready to look at the world through the eyes of people whose lives are turning into a living hell?
