## 简介（VNDB 原文）

<!-- AUTO:BEGIN synopsis -->
"Please look, Mother. The town, our town, is burning."
"Why is this happening... Oh, I'm sorry. While the city you cherished, Glia, is being set ablaze, all I can do is watch..."
A woman with beautiful silver hair, likely a mother and daughter, gazes from the mansion window at the city being set on fire and the repeated looting by the invading forces. In just three days, the soldiers of the former Goras Kingdom, now turned enemies, approach the mansion while engaging in arson.

"Mother, I'm scared."
"It's okay, Misha. I'm here with you."

Gently embracing her daughter, the beautiful silver-haired woman answers. Her name is Almina Grodnoi, the Lady who inherited the lush regional city of Glia from her deceased husband. The silver-haired girl, snuggling close in fear of Almina, is named Misha Grodnoi, Almina's daughter with an equally beautiful appearance as her mother.

As the Goras Revolutionary Army surrounds the mansion, Almina wears a face of despair. Rather than getting captured, she contemplates ending her life here with her daughter. However, Orié, Misha's personal maid, who served as a guide, appears before the resigned Almina and delivers a message.

"Leave the mansion using the underground passage, and head to the neighboring country, Altani, with the carriage I've prepared."

Confronting Almina's hesitation to abandon the citizens and flee alone, Orié points out her duty as a Lady to rebuild the city and the obligation as a mother to protect Misha. Reluctantly following Orié's guidance through the underground waterway, the trio escapes from the mansion and heads towards the territory of Remnant Ortia, the late husband's close friend, in Altani.

Having survived and reached Altani safely, Almina, Misha, and Orié, who narrowly escaped death, hide in Remnant's castle, "Vezel Castle," for a while. Almina, with a kind and motherly personality, possesses a beautiful appearance and a voluptuous, mature body. Misha, inheriting her mother's beauty, exudes a delicate and youthful charm.

Orié, with oriental black hair and long ears as an elf descendant, and a trained body befitting a former warrior, holds undisclosed affection, pure adoration, and a debt that must be repaid.

The feelings the three have for Remnant, though varying in intensity, are all affectionate. Living together with three exceptional women with striking beauty and enticing bodies, their distances naturally decrease, and they begin to intertwine both physically and emotionally.
