## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The game presents the life of an ordinary 21-year-old guy, specifically Nikita Komyakin. He seems like someone no different from other Russians, but over time, certain strange things begin to happen in his life, which the player will have to experience alongside the main character. Drama, comedy, lowbrow and dark humor, sometimes even complete absurdity... "The Last Rave" contains all of this. Although the visual novel is humorous, you should not take everything as pure comedy, because there are just as many secrets here as there are jokes.
