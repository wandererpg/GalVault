## 简介（VNDB 原文）

You play a university student named Fuji Touya. Your long-term girlfriend, Morikawa Yuki, is starting to become a very popular idol singer. Unfortunately, her popularity begins to pull the two of you apart; you must either try to maintain your relationship with her or find someone else.
