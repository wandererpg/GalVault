## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The game centers around you, a heartbroken man with a background in cryptocurrency trading, telling the story of how you met a girl named Megan during a life changing day.

Megan is a young aspiring actress down on her luck and in desperate need for help. Will you be there for her when she needs it the most and help her turn the tide? Will you be able to win her heart?
