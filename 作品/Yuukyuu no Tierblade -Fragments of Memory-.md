## 简介（VNDB 原文）

A fandisk of Yuukyuu no Tierblade -Lost Chronicle-.

-Eclipse-
Another story set after the true ending of the original game.

-Future-
After stories for the main characters set after their happy endings
in the original game.

- Fragment-
Details events from the perspective of other characters
revealing things that could not be told before.
