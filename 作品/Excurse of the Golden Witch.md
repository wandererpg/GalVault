## 简介（VNDB 原文）

In October of 1987, a 28-year old stateroom steward calling herself Lunon returns to southern Japan for a reunion with her three younger "sisters" after more than a year abroad. In the wake of  an impossible occult library and  mysterious, seaborne message finding their way into the public eye, a bygone piece of the sisters' history arises to the fore... and Lunon faces off with a unique challenge from the Golden Witch addressed directly to her.
