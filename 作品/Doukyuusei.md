## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Doukyusei is a classic dating-sim where you play as a high school student during his last summer vacation of high school and has fourteen girls to pick to have a romantic relationship with.

To do so  with a little luck and effort, you will have to  meet with them, talk to them, date them, to capture the heart of the girl of your choice. But be careful for you are not the only one who is pursuing some of the girls.

Diligence is important, However there's no harm in also making as many woman as you can fall for you right?
