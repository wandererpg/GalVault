## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Waking up in a hospital room with an unfamiliar ceiling, Shinji can't remember what exactly happened before he got to the doctors. Driven by the desire to find out the reason for what is happening around him, he leaves the room and becomes an accidental witness to mysterious and incomprehensible events.
