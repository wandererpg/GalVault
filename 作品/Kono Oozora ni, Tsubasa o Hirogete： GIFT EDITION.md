## 简介（VNDB 原文）

A short visual novel that comes as bonus from purchasing the series' 10th anniversary tapestry or acrylic set.
Taking place after the graduation in Flight Diary, GIFT EDITION illustrates one scene in a certain day in summer, where the old members are gathering at the Flying Fish Manor and spend their days off.
There are some changes in the story depending on Kotori side or Ageha side.
