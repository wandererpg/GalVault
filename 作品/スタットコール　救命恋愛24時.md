## 简介（VNDB 原文）

<!-- AUTO:BEGIN synopsis -->
You play as a brand new surgeon assigned to the elite ICU team at a university hospital in downtown Tokyo. Mentored by one of five brilliant, handsome surgeons, you receive strict personal guidance, all the while discovering that the true life of a doctor is a far cry from the ideals you expected. The smiles of your patients and the rare glimpse of kindness from your mentor are enough to keep you going and help you grow. In these tales of love, the bond between you and your senior surgeon are nurtured at the forefront of healthcare, where mistakes cannot be afforded and hurdles must be overcome.
