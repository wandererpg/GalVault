## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Kyousuke comes to Riki’s classroom one day, telling him something terrible has happened. He takes him to another room and they discover a truly awful sight…

Mio Nishizono’s corpse.

No, she isn’t actually dead. But there’s something very strange about this, namely the fact that Mio was "murdered" in a locked room. Riki is promptly tasked with solving the mystery of how this could have been possible.

Mission Start!

[From [Rincchi's blog](https://ritobasu.wordpress.com/2016/01/25/nishizono-mios-locked-room-murder-mystery/)]

This game was released with the first Blu-ray Disc volume for the Little Busters! Refrain anime.
