## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This game was released with the fifth DVD and Blu-ray Disc volume for the Grisaia no Rakuen anime. It contains several short stories taking place somewhere during Grand Route of Grisaia no Meikyuu, revealing some interesting facts about Yuuji's and Asako's past.
