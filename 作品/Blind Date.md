## 简介（VNDB 原文）

A mod for "I Wani Hug that Gator!" Blind Date follows the story of Bianca and Niko, each stuck in their own worlds, both trying to fulfill their desires.

Taking place several years after the main game, you now play as Niko, an avid skateboarder attempting to find and create a new social circle at St. Hammond in his senior year. All so that he is able to become popular to become the next Roary Hawk.

You will meet and make many friends, but one of them is Bianca, a blind raptor and talented sculptor. Once again, your choices matter in order to become popular and well respected at your new art school.

[From [itch.io](https://kailoulou.itch.io/blind-date)]
