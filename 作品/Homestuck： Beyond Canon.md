## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Homestuck: Beyond Canon is the official successor to critically acclaimed visual novel [Homestuck](/v31659). It continues directly from the end of both routes of its sequel webnovel, [The Homestuck Epilogues](https://www.homestuck.com/epilogues).

The initial demo release ("Homestuck^2") went on hiatus on December 25, 2020. The full version picked up where it left off on October 8, 2023, with an entirely new staff.
