## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Boyfriend to Death is a murdersim game originally intended as a joke between friends. It's structured like a dating sim but with a dark twist as you try to survive a few nights with these gentlemen. It contains heavy tones of rape, torture, death and other dark and twisted elements. This game is not intended for the faint of heart.

[Edited from [official site](https://web.archive.org/web/20160705070509/http://boyfriendtodeath.com/index.html)]
