## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This event occurs before the girl by the name of King Arthur passes away. Before she can make a contract with the world and before the 4th holy grail war…the continuation of a dream.

As the girl lay in a coma, she had a flashback of her life - the days of King Arthur and the knights of the round, those glorious days of her past.

'What is your wish?'

An unknown voice drew her back to relive history. Upon revisiting her past, can she find the answer that she seeks?

This time, I can change my fate.
