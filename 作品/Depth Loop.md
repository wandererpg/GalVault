## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Pushed to the brink of extinction by a pandemic, humanity's last hope lies in a deep-sea facility.

But when a werewolf infiltrates the crew, the brilliant scientists are thrust into a deadly game of suspicion.

Can you unravel the mystery of Depth Loop and secure humanity's future?

As a player, join the Werewolf game!

------

The game features twelve genius researchers from around the world, along with the player, werewolves, mermaids, and a commander.

Under orders from the commander, the player is dispatched to the deep-sea special facility “Mag Mell,” where they must participate in Werewolf games with the researchers to uncover its mysteries.

Depending on the outcome of each Werewolf game, the story progresses, gradually revealing the characters’ pasts and the secrets of “Mag Mell.”
