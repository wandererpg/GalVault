## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Why venture into a desolate city, shrouded in rumors and whispers? You stumbled onto the legend of this ghost town in 2019, a mere campfire tale dismissed as fiction. Yet, an inexplicable tug pulled you here, to this forgotten corner of the world. The silence is heavy, the air thick with a sense of unease. As you explore the crumbling structures, a chilling realization dawns – some truths were better left buried.

Cycles is a visual novel that delves into the lure of the unknown and the chilling consequences of curiosity. Step into the shoes of a skeptic forced to confront a reality beyond their wildest nightmares. Will you unravel the mysteries of this haunted city, or become another victim of its endless cycle?
