## 简介（VNDB 原文）

"What would have happened if that accident never took place?"

Kimi ga Nozomu Eien Special Fan Disc explores this concept by retelling the first chapter of the original game. Also includes radio shows, puzzles, wallpapers and other fan materials.

The puzzle game Marie's Cleanup 2 - AyuMayu's Struggle Edition (マリーのおかたづけ2 あゆまゆ奮闘編 -) is also included.
