## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Takiyo Mochizuki is accepted into Heaven’s Bridge Boarding School, Japan’s most elite academy. Recently renowned for molding talented individuals into Ultimates — skilled professionals in their desired fields — Heaven’s Bridge accepts students between the ages of 10-20 in its pursuit of notable alumni destined to make their mark on Japanese history. After an unfortunate tragedy forced the school into a temporary shutdown, it has now reopened, and intends to restart its prestigious legacy with its 21st class. Unbeknownst to them, another tragedy is quickly approaching.
