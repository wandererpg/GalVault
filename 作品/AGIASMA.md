Do you believe in God? How do you feel about religions and spiritual traditions? Do you think there is any meaning in all of this? And will it lead to positive consequences?

If you have ever been interested in these questions, I invite you to follow the development of our Danganronpa fan visual novel titled "AGIASMA". Here you will witness the daily life of an ordinary girl who decided to spend her entire life in prayer, harmony, and complete peace, protected by the Almighty. But it seems that things will not go quite the way she would have liked...

How do you think this will all end?
