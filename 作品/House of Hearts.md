## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The House of Hearts.
A place where its customers are served with their deepest wishes. A place where aspiring demons nurture their strength within the safety of the House's creator.

The ones within fight to reach the Hall of Fame, an invaluable title reserved for the House's best performers; demons who know which and what button to press, humans who know what face they must show.

And now, it accepts its newest entry: You. A demon out of time, with memories sealed by the one who created you. Your purpose: to save the life of a beloved, to mend the soul of a broken spirit. The road ahead will be perilous: your goals deemed blasphemous by the demon kind.
But you are not alone. Sponsored by Asmodeus, you and your allies arrived in a world of lust and seduction.

Will you reach the Hall of Fame before time runs out?
