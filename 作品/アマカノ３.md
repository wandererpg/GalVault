## 简介（VNDB 原文）

<!-- AUTO:BEGIN synopsis -->
“I have come to fetch you.”

Out of the blue one day, a maid by the name of Scarlet Ikaruga Wisteria arrived at my doorstep,
proclaiming to be the adopted daughter of my grandmother.

My grandmother had recently been hospitalized due to poor health,
and so had requested for my help in managing the dormitory that she ran.

Though I had my reservations,
I ultimately accepted her proposal to work at Sasaura Dorm—a girls’ dormitory.

Otori Sayumi, kind and well-liked classmate of the protagonist.
Kanon Yanagi, a cheerful and spunky gyaru one year the protagonist’s junior.
Shion Yanagi, Kanon’s older sister who refuses to accept the protagonist’s existence at the dorm.
Finally, with the addition of the newly transferred Toka Goshoin—heiress to the Azarashi zaibatsu group,
it’s become quite the challenge for the protagonist to integrate into this new environment.

But as they start living together under one roof, the protagonist begins to grow closer with his housemates, getting to know their hopes and dreams as the curtain rises on this story of summertime youth in this hot-spring town.

[From [Shiravune](https://shiravune.com/archives/11306)]
