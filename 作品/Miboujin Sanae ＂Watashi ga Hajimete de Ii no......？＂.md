## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Hello, my name is Inukai Tsukasa. I was born as the only son of a real estate agent in a declining business district. I'm an average student in both my studies and in sports. I've personally never had a girlfriend and so I'm still embarrassingly inexperienced. But it would be a little wrong to say I'm ambivalent to girls as I'm very open to older women. Also, my mother works as the head of her women's association. She has me help her with her work there, and I think everyone might be doting on me a little. I mean, is it appropriate at this age for me to be getting scolded and teased by them? I get picked on, but within that there's a special privilege to compensate for it, because all the women I long for are there. And by way of an unexpected occurrence I came to know their acceptingness and their hidden loneliness. From that day, I couldn't clear those women from my mind. This is the story of how I came to love those kinds of older women.
