## 简介（VNDB 原文）

An original set of extra stories set in the TM2 timeline, inspired by the Drama Series from TM1 but with different gameplay.

MRO is set one year after graduation, with the protagonist remembering his school days at a coffee shop. He mostly focuses on his last Valentine's Day in high school, and how several girls acted that one day.
