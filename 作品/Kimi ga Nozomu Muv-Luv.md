Fandisc released for free to members of the age Fan Club, Kimi ga Nozomu Muv-Luv contains two stories:
-Ore ga Hetare de Hetare ga Ore de:
A crossover for [Kimi ga Nozomu Eien](/v94) and [Muv-Luv](/v93) where the protagonists for each game swap bodies, and wacky hijinks ensue!
-Melvina Maniax
A short story about daily life of the JFK Curry Restoraunt crew.
