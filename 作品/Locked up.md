## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Yoshi is an 18-year-old guy with a bleak future. Despite a serious illness, he has little to complain about following his enrollment at Yamaku. With a girlfriend, a solid circle of friends, and wealthy parents, his life is defined by comfort rather than responsibility. Lazy and driftless, Yoshi is content to simply go with the flow, never giving a second thought to what lies ahead.

That is, until a chance encounter brings him together with Kyouko — a girl living in a world of eternal darkness and silence. Her presence forces him to confront a reality he never imagined, and a choice that will change his life forever.
