## 简介（VNDB 原文）

An alternative read of [New Dangan Ronpa V3 Minna no Koroshiai Shingakki](/v18334). "Reaching" sets out to explore the idea of time-travel within the series. Death is the end of the line... usually. However, when Ouma Kokichi died, he just came right back. Right back into that same locker he was stuffed into the first time.

This novel follows him as he tries to prevent the same outcome from repeating. Just how much can you take when you roll the die again and again?
