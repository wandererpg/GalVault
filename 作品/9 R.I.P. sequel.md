## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Heavenly Tales:
A dark and mysterious love story unfolds across four chapters, beginning from the previous ending, "Finale: Heaven."
Even after that conclusion, lingering shadows remain.
Various forces close in on the heroine and her beloved, throwing their future into chaos.
In the darkness, the two move forward, guided only by their trust in each other.

Godly Tales:
A continuation of the previous ending, "Finale: God," told through four seasonal episodes.
Each chapter highlights a moment that left a lasting impression on the heroine and those close to her.
These stories, shaped by the passing seasons, reveal the bonds they’ve built together.
A heartfelt journey through memories, leading to the present shared by the two.
