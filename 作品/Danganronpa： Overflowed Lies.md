## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The game follows Kaiyo Aino, the Ultimate Novelist attending her first year at Hope's Peak. However, this is slowly ruined as the freshman class of Hope’s Peak has been kidnapped by a well-known serial killer, who has an unknown goal involving the school and symbols of talent. Throughout Kaiyo's not-so-fun schoolyear, the featured cast uncovers more about their captor and how to escape the clutches of the killing game.
