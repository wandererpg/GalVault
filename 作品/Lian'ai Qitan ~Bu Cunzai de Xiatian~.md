## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The person behind the Vtuber was dead!? Members of the Occult Club came across a video showing how the person behind Vtuber Yumeno Hitomi was murdered. The Vtuber featuring mostly urban legends was still going live even after the real world person was dead. Gu Wei and his girlfriend Su Banxia of the Occult Club decided to investigate. What would they find out?
