## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Luna used to lounge on the sofa watching pre-recorded cupcake documentaries or spend time chatting with his BFF (rubber ducky) in the jacuzzi. But ever since he agreed to his twin imouto Corona’s request to make everyone happy, he has no time for that! He’s been busy selling homemade ramune with his osananajimi Lamune, hanging out at the Cherry Crown Diner run by his friends Rayla and Iris, and going out on road trips over the weekends (with Corona and him taking turns at the wheel). Together with his friends and lover, this isn’t just happiness… it’s perfection!

The theme of the game is ‘”All Ways” Be Happy’, depicting a happy and carefree lifestyle. It’s mainly a comedy set in the resort town Saint Aria, where magic and technology co-exist.
