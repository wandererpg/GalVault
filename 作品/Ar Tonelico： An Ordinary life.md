## 简介（VNDB 原文）

A fanfiction that later was adapted on Ren'py telling the story of a nameless male protagonist who lives and studies in a world full of AT series characters.

This game is completely unrelated to the creators of the AT series.

WARNING: This game contains some MAJOR spoilers to both AT1 and AT2. It is recomended to play it only if you already played both games of the original series.

[Project site](http://artonelico.isisview.org/wiki/index.php/Ar_tonelico:_An_Ordinary_Life/Visual_Novel)
