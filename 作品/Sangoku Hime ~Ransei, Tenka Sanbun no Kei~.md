## 简介（VNDB 原文）

There can only be one dragon (emperor), however heaven sent a 3-headed dragon to the world. Soon an uprising called the Yellow Turban Rebellion happened, marking the beginning of a battle for supremacy. This is known as the period of the Three Kingdoms.

[From [Hau~ Omochikaeri!](https://omochikaeri.wordpress.com/2010/12/11/december-2010-eroge-releases/)]

Following Ginga, a mysterious man that joined Ryuubi Gentoku, Sousou Moutouku and Sonken Bundai in the battle against the Yellow Turbans and his struggling adventures in this chaotic era.
