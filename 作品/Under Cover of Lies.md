## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The events of the game unfold in the gloomy and cold streets of Chelyabinsk, where tenth grader Vlad Lenin lives.

He is an ordinary C student and an introvert, convinced that he does not need anyone. His days pass in gray monotony, and his evenings are filled with heavy thoughts and memories. Traumas from the past have left a mark on his soul, amplifying his isolation and alienation.

Vlad does not stand out among his peers. But in his heart, pain and longing grow, which he hides behind a mask of indifference. The only light in his life is his childhood friend, his exact opposite.

Vlad is hopelessly in love with him, but the fear of ruining their relationship holds him back from confessing. In every glance, unexpressed feelings are hidden, and every word comes with difficulty.

An unexpected internet acquaintance with a person under a mysterious nickname could change his entire life.

Will this connection be able to pull Vlad out of the abyss of loneliness, or will it lead to even greater chaos in his life?
