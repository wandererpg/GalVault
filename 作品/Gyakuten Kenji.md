## 简介（VNDB 原文）

In this *Ace Attorney* spin-off game, taking place between *Phoenix Wright: Ace Attorney - Trials and Tribulations* and *Apollo Justice: Ace Attorney*, prosecutor Miles Edgeworth investigates a series of cases related to an international smuggling ring. He is assisted by detective Dick Gumshoe and self-proclaimed "Great Thief" Kay Faraday, while his investigations also lead him to cross paths with Interpol agent Shi-Long Lang.
