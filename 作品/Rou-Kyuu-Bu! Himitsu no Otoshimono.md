## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Subaru, the girls' basketball club and Takenaka are staying at Keishin Elementary School in order to train for a competition. However, Subaru keeps finding the girls' panties in all sorts of places! Can he return them all before they notice?

This game features a simplified training system compared to the first one, and ditches the basketball matches in favor of a real-time sneaking minigame.
