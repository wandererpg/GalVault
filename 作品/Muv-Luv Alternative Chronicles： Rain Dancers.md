## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Rain Dancers was originally a [Muv-Luv Alternative](/v92) fanfiction written by [Wei Luxin](/s7978).
It was officially recognized by Age and published by them during the [âge x Nitro+ Kanzen Gattai DX Godo Akiba Event](https://www.nitroplus.co.jp/pc/event/ditailed/081227/index.html), the 27 December 2008, as part of a fanbook called MUV-LUV ALTERNATIVE LD2 PROJECT PROMINENCE.
Later, Chicken Divers was made into a visual novel which was released at Comiket 77, which took place during December 2009 (29-31). Unfortunately, this release was full of bugs.
The 30 July 2010, it was republished inside Chronicles 01, bundled with the databook MUV-LUV ALTERNATIVE LD3 Code：Rebellion. Voices were added and the production value enhanced.

Rain Dancers is a short story that takes place in November 1995. It details part of the effort of a combined European force using the brand new test EF-2000 Typhoons to create a foothold in Europe by retaking parts of Italy from BETA control.

[Synopsis from the [Muv-Luv Fandom Wiki](https://muvluv.fandom.com/wiki/Muv-Luv_Alternative_Chronicles_Rain_Dancers)]
