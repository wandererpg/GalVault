## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Ex-delinquent Tobimura Eigo has his first day of receiving his new job as teacher ruined by the rampage of a mysterious group of evil woman executives. To make it worse, his next job at Otoki school is stolen by some random guy introduced himself as God of Education's descendant. Devastated, he goes to the schoolyard just to get involved in the flashy battle between those hateful evil woman executives and the superhero named Bakken Loader.

After twists and turns, he ends up as spy impersonating that God of Education guy who was supposed to join the evil organization behind the school on the same day. Together with the superhero Bakken Loader and the divine beast of God of Education, he decides to use his new power to overthrow the evil mastermind which takes root in this school by "Educating" those woman executives.
