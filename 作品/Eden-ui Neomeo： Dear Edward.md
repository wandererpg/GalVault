## 简介（VNDB 原文）

What happened after "Beyond Eden" when Alex decided to abandon his quest for revenge against the Edenics?

The gullible and callow aristocrat Edward winds up with 1000 pounds in gambling debt.
But rather than own up to his stern elder brother, Edward chooses a 1-year loan from his crush Alex.
In return, Edward agrees to pay Alex back while working for his company in London.
Can Edward pay off his debts within a year, and also confess his feelings to Alex?
Will Edward be of any use at all to Alex, considering that he has never worked a day in his life?
Can Edward quit gambling after so many years of addiction?
Is it true that the London town house where he resides is haunted?
And why does Edward avoid reuniting with his former classmate from boarding school?
What kind of man will he become?

Studio Pieplus delivers you this sweet and fresh romance—either with a confident and elegant gentleman, or an affectionate and adorable alumnus.
