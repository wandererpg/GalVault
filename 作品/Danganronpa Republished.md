## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Danganronpa Republished follows the son of a famous poet and a renowned journalist, Yuu Takami, as he is plunged onto an island of despair, trapped in a killing game! Including him, 17 students fight for their lives and endure the likes of suffering they didn't know imaginable before. Love triangles and petty drama will distract the cast of young adults from the situation at hand, and Yuu will find himself caught between a rock and a hard place as they struggle to escape. Which students of the class will live to tell and publish the story of Danganronpa Republished?
