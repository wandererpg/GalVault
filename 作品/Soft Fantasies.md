Welcome to "Soft Fantasies" A furry visual novel focused on the topic of gaining weight. Where you take the role of a little chubby puppy who has just moved to a new place to study in a new academy. In this new life you will find a great variety of characters with different personalities and tastes. You decide what will happen in the end depending on the wide range of decisions to be made for each chapter. Gaining or losing weight. There will be many different endings. Try to get the one that suits you best.
