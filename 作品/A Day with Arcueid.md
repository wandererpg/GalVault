## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The story apparently is about Arcued waking you up to wish you a happy birthday, regardless of whether or not it is. However, is that really happening?

In "A Day with Arcueid" you will find a story that progressively mutates with each game and dialogue you choose. Can you discover the truth? Will you be able to reveal all its secrets? Will you manage to get the final 14?

Game link: https://deadlysmile.itch.io/a-day-with-arcueid
