## 简介（VNDB 原文）

<!-- AUTO:BEGIN synopsis -->
The story takes place in a picturesque provincial town, where a series of mysterious incidents begin to unfold.

A young girl’s ghost climbing onto rooftops every night.
Foxfire flickering in the shrine.
A demon lurking in the mountain pass, luring people in.
Disappearances tied to a fifty-year-old mystery.
All these mysteries converge into a single message:

“Let’s solve all these mysteries together!”

Join Haruta, a boy from Tokyo, and Yukiko, a tengu girl, in this heartwarming coming-of-age love comedy mixed with small-town mystery!

The coming winter will be their last.
This is the story of a boy and a girl, racing through their final moments together, living vividly in the present.
