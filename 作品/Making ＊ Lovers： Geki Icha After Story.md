## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The fandisc for Making＊Lovers, includes the after story that takes part over a year later for each heroine.

Ako is involved in family fight with her father, and starts competing with him over who can seduce the protagonist better!
Saki is enjoying her happy life living together with protagonist, when a new assailant: mass-produced Hamjiro appears! Meanwhile, Saki's mother comes to visit!
Karen and the protagonist once again gather for a mixer with the same members... and Karen takes her revenge against everyone who pissed her off by showing off!
After over a year apart, and protagonist returning back to Japan later than expected, Reina has evolved into a Black Reina out of loneliness!
As for Mashiro... her and the protagonist's workplace is taken over by Evil Homar Virus!
