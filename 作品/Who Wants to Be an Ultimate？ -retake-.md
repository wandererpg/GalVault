## 简介（VNDB 原文）

A person who was considered by the Ultimate Scouting Organization as a possible Ultimate title holder who didn't end up receiving it.

"Who Wants to Be an Ultimate?" was meant to be their second chance to earn an Ultimate title. A reality show where the world would be able to see their worth, a place where they would be allowed to compete directly against the Ultimates. However, 8 Nulltimates and 7 Ultimates would find themselves trapped in a mysterious hotel and forced to play a killing game by two mascots, naming themselves the showrunners, who tell them their only way to escape is to kill one of their fellow cast members.
