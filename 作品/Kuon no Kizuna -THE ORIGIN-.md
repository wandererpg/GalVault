## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This is the fully-voiced remake of FOG/XUSE’s 1998 title of the same name. It adds a new scenario and also additional events for existing routes. With the arrival of new transfer student Mayou, Takeru is caught up in bizarre incidents and has unexplainable nightmares. He hasn’t realized yet that it is related to his destiny from thousands of years ago, in the Heian Era.

[From [Hau~ Omochikaeri!](http://omochikaeri.wordpress.com/2011/07/01/july-2011-eroge-releases/)]
