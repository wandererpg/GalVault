## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The story begins with a boy named Lyosha, who moves from a big city to a small town due to family circumstances. Upon starting school there, he discovers that everything written on the internet about the town and its strange occurrences is actually true.

Despite this, our main character makes three good friends, and together they form a close group. Over time, when Lyosha decides to grow closer to one specific friend, he learns about his friend's personal problem and tries his absolute best to help them overcome their life struggles.
