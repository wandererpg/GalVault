## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This is a strip mahjong game with characters from four Elf games (3 girls from each game) and 10 girls from Jan Jaka Jan. You have to defeat each game's male protectors in order to play with that game's girls.

[From [Li's Fun House](https://web.archive.org/web/20150502131520/http://members.shaw.ca:80/lisun/jhag-wna.htm)]
