## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This compilation includes three sets of stories:

- "After Stories"
Various after-stories focused on relationships with six heroines, added in D.C.II P.C. Sexual scenes are included.
- "Kazami Educational Institution Drama Club -Autumn Premiere-"
Movies created by school drama club with Anzu Yukimura as a scenarist/director and her classmates as actors. Some horror, drama and frags are guaranteed.
- "Ultimate Battle!"
Internecine wars between classmates of the protagonist, which occur during the school cultural and sports festivals. You can even hear the singing of some heroines here. And there is also the story about sexual harassment of Yoshiyuki's sisters.
