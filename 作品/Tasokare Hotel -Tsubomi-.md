## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Will this journey take you back to the real world, or to hell?


When he opened his eyes, there was nothing but open plains.
He couldn’t remember how he got there, or even what his name was.
If only it were a dream.
With that hope in mind he began to walk, when he came across a mysterious young man called Yuran.

“Who are you?”
“You don’t remember. Then you must be a guest.”
“Come with me.”

Guided by Yuran, he arrived at a retro-looking building known as the “Tasogare Hotel.”
Inside, it was full of guests who all had strange, deformed heads.
When Yuran mentions that he looked just as unique, he realised for the first time that something was wrong with his head too.

“Welcome to the Tasogare Hotel.”

Will our protagonist be able to regain his memories and leave the hotel?
