## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Chitose Haruki's life was anything but ordinary.
His phone took a swim every time he dropped it, his wallet seemed to have a disappearing act of its own, bike accidents were a regular occurrence, and even a simple walk down the street could turn into a confrontation.

While others might consider these events heartbreaking, for Haruki, they were just another Tuesday. He had made peace with his perpetually bad luck and found a strange sense of harmony within the chaos.

Then, one day, his life took a bizarre turn.
A mysterious package landed on his doorstep – no sender, no address, just a label that said "living thing." Talk about a head-scratcher, even for someone like Haruki. And what was inside? A girl, believe it or not.

This unexpected arrival wasn't another misfortune, but rather, his first stroke of luck, albeit a chaotic one. The girl claimed to be a "god" sent to eliminate Haruki's bad luck, and she promptly decided to move in, much to his surprise.

However, her presence was like adding gasoline to a fire. Minor mishaps snowballed into full-blown fiascos, pulling everyone around them into the mayhem.

So, what will this "lucky break" bring to Haruki's chaotic world?
What's the deal with this self-proclaimed deity?
And will Haruki finally ditch his bad luck streak?

Buckle up, because the answers lie ahead in the wild ride that's about to unfold!
