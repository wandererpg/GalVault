## 简介（VNDB 原文）

A collection of four fangames based off the series "Umineko no Naku Koro ni":

Fan-EP1: Forgery of the Golden Witch
Fan-EP2: Trick of the Golden Witch
Fan-EP3: Inquisition of the Golden Witch
Fan-EP4: Board of the Golden Witch

Each game contains a mystery, with answers to them revealed in their corresponding Tea Parties. While EP1 and EP2 are standard kinetic novels, EP3 becomes an interactive adventure game, allowing you to become the detective and solve the mystery yourself! EP4 is also vastly different from the previous games, but returns the story to kinetic novel format.
