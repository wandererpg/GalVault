## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Keiji Shinogi is a rookie policeman who may not know exactly what he wants. The job comes with many demands and a few unexpected turns. But perhaps, that's what makes a job like this worth it: to be a beacon of hope and justice.

But do laws justify the harsh punishments that are inflicted not only on the body, but even on one's heart?

Megumi is an ideal mentor who keeps an eye on her pupils. Angeline, a police captain who distances herself from the full scope of her duty.

And a man fated to carry responsibility without knowing what it fully entails.

Without knowing about the other forces that will soon come to play with their lives.

The ones who attempt to take a seat... and to judge their fate.
