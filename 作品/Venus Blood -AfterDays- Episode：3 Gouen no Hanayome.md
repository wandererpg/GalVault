## 简介（VNDB 原文）

After the war on Helvetia, Leonhardt has successfully united the land under his banner, and spends his time in Archlond with his thoroughly awakened 'bride', Julia von Archlond.

However, she no longer acts like a proud dragon, but has instead fallen to the level of a slovenly housecat, spending her every waking moment chasing her husband around seeking his attention.

Getting somewhat sick of her current state, Leonhardt begins to think of a plan to get her off his back, a form of 're-education', if you will, and who better to help him with this plan than Julia's own beloved younger sister, Liese.
