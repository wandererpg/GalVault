## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Shake It: Lycoris is a bartender simulator where cozy vibes, smooth jazz, and a wide variety of cocktails blend into one unique atmosphere.

Lycoris is more than just a place to drink. It’s a bar where guests arrive with stories of joy and sorrow, worries and dreams. Listen to them, share the moment, and craft the perfect cocktail for every occasion.

You step behind the counter of Lycoris almost by accident—and perhaps without any bartending experience. If you know your way around a bar in real life, you’ll feel right at home. If words like “jigger” and “shaker” only ever came from movies, don’t worry—you’ll be learning the craft of mixology from the ground up.
