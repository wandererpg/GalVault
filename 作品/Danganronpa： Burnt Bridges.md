## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Can YOU get away with murder?

8 university students have been trapped inside Hope’s Peak Academy, with the only way of escape being to commit murder and get away with it in a class trial. Following the aftermath of the first murder and trial, you play as Bernie Stokes, who devises his own murder plan.

Stalk your classmates. Pick a victim. Frame someone else for your crime.

Will Bernie be able to survive the class trial and escape Hope’s Peak Academy… or will he get exposed as the culprit?
