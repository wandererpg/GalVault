## 简介（VNDB 原文）

In 201X, in Tokyo’s largest entertainment district, K-town…

Meet Fukami Daigo, a man who loves groping more than his three square meals a day.
Operating out of a small bar called COLORS in a corner of K-town, he runs a side business as a “Groping Specialist.”
His job? Taking requests from men who hold grudges against women and exacting revenge through groping—for a fee, of course.

“I got scammed by a woman. Help me out.”
“I want revenge on the woman who ruined my life.”
“I want to teach that arrogant woman a lesson.”

Men with all sorts of grievances, grudges, and hatred come to COLORS.
And today, just like any other day, Daigo—the “Groping Avenger”—begins his “work,” blending his personal interests with a profitable business.
