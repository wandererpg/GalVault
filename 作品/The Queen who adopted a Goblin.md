## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The Kingdom of Golden Kine has just won a great battle against a great horde of goblins. As the king proudly surveyed the aftermath of the battlefield with his beloved, they find a lone goblin survivor within one of the destroyed catapults. The Queen takes an interest in him and decides to adopt him in order to discover and learn how humans and goblins could peacefully co-exist. Thus her "discovery" begins and her son will be the witness..​.
