## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Originally an additional story included at the end of Twofold, Perfect Circle is a story about love, connection and what we leave behind.

Perfect Circle: Stand Alone is an enhanced edition of this short story featuring:

-An additional scene
-Enhanced UI
-A brand new music track

[From the [official website](https://2nd-draft.itch.io/perfect-circle)]
