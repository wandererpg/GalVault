## 简介（VNDB 原文）

In a certain continent of a world that is not here, the large-scale war was unfolding across the entire continent after the fall of the unified Gilan Empire. Amidst this, Adel Orcus led a large army of beastmen, mainly composed of orcs, attacking cities and countries, assaulting women, devouring food and resources without a trace, and causing successive destruction. However, his fate changed drastically when he invaded the lakeside country of Carlet.
He brutally humiliates and kills Queen Seno Eitos and then reaches out to her daughter, Levina. However, her hidden power is temporarily released, and Adel is defeated by its overwhelming force. It took four years for the wounds he received from Levina to fully heal. During that time, Adel was secretly preparing for his revenge on Levina. However, those four years also intensified Levina Eitos' desire for revenge.
Fueled by the thirst for revenge, it was enough time for her to gain the strength to defeat the man who had brutally killed her mother, family, and people. And their passions collide on the stage of the royal capital, Cruyff.
The curtain of tyranny rises once again here and now.
