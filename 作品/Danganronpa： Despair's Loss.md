## 简介（VNDB 原文）

A school where despair will reign, and 15 students will find themselves in a mutual game of killing. Sayaka Maizono, Leon Kutawa, Chihiro Fujisaki, Mondo Owada, Kiyotaka Ishimaru, Celestia Ludenberg, Yasuhiro Hagakure, Hifumi Yamada, Toko Fukawa, Aoi Asahina, Sakura Ogami, Byakuya Togami, Kyoko Kirigiri, Junko Enoshima, and Makoto Naegi. Will our familiar characters live to see the outside world? Do they even want to see the outside world? Let's just pray they never have to go through a deadly defence, a deadly riddle, a deadly....class trial.
