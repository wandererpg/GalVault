## 简介（VNDB 原文）

In the middle of the mountains stands an isolated, rural hot springs inn.

There works the beautiful waitress, Inamori Haru.

Kamijou Souta, the lover of Haru, secretly pays a visit to the inn in order to surprise her...

This is the story of a night of passionate love woven by a foolish couple of adults who have fallen in love...
