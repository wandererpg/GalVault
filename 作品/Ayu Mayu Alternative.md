## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Ayu Mayu Alternative is mainly about the events taking place around [Daikuuji Ayu](/c37397) and [Tamano Mayu](/c37396), the two waitresses from Sky Temple.

It is inspired by the anime Ayu Mayu Gekijou which had its fourth episode as a parody of Muv-Luv Alternative.
