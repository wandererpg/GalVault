## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Spring, the season of new encounters――

You enrolled at Hoshimi Academy. In your new class, you are assigned a seat next to a girl. Her name is Irokawa Suzune, she doesn't like to socialize, and even though you tried to talk to her, she put you off.

One day after school, you saw an impressive scene that you will never forget. The girl is sitting alone in the classroom, playing a guitar, with the sunset in the background. She is doing her best at what she likes. Seeing her like that, you gradually become attracted to her.

This is a story of youth full of "like" and "love".
