## 简介（VNDB 原文）

A legend is circulating around the Internet.

"All music bands of a specific genre that possess incredible potential are cursed. When faced with poor mental health and a lack of confidence, 'something' forces the band members to make risky moves and do everything possible to sabotage their own success, gradually plunging them into deeper despair."

The story begins in the past. A group of friends, affectionately known by locals as "Metalheads, stupid alternative kids," is returning from rehearsal. As usual, ignoring the biting remarks and judgmental stares of passersby, the group thinks: "Why not hit the movie theater? We only ever see each other at rehearsals anyway."

The moment they deviate from their usual route, a heavy downpour begins. They completely did not expect such a sudden change in the weather, and while discussing what to do next, they notice their lead vocalist has vanished. This is highly unusual because she is always up for any adventure.

The group has no idea what happened. Their singer is not answering her phone, so the search begins. At this exact moment, the internet legend naturally invented by Haters makes its move. But perhaps things are not as simple as they appear?
