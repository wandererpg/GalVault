## 简介（VNDB 原文）

This is the fandisk for Owaru Sekai to Birthday and Futagoza no Paradox. It includes after and side stories, some bonus H-scenes and a minigame. Most importantly, it features a full route for chara poll victor Misaki, where she becomes Touya’s childhood friend and Ili is his real younger sister.
