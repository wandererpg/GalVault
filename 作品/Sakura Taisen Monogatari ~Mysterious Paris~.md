## 简介（VNDB 原文）

A visual novel set a year after Sakura Taisen 3. A dancer has disappeared at Chatnoir Theatre, and the Japanese detective Akechi Kojirou is tasked with the case. He will be supported by the Hanagumi and his younger sister, Miki.
