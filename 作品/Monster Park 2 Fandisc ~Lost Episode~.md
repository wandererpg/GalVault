## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The fandisk of monster park 2 contains

-  Appending Scenario -
Expand the main game with over 40 scenes which were cut because of disk space shortage. Also contains a special scenario "The banquet of half-fish men".
* Appending Scenario requires original monster park 2 to be installed in your pc.

- Guest Gallery -
5 original monsters drawn by 5 different guest artists with their unique "characteristics"  and scenes.

- Side story -
New side stories for 3 popular heroines - Scarlett, Rukamira and Mari.

- The world of Monsterpark -
Encyclopedia of various stuffs used during development of monster park 2.
