## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Danieru Makishisu, an ordinary high school student with an extraordinary stroke of luck, had written a letter to the prestigious and newly established "Symbol of Hope" Lyceum. Thanks to the Talent Development Program, he was granted the opportunity to develop his hidden talent in the name of prosperity... and Hope. Or so he thought. The second he set foot inside the Lyceum, something irreversible happened. He wasn’t at the entrance ceremony. Danieru was trapped in a place that seemed to twist the very idea of Hope into something far more unsettling...
