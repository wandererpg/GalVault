## 简介（VNDB 原文）

The plot of the modification is similar to the original novel: the main character, high school student Hisao Nakai, is transferred to a boarding school for children with disabilities “Yamaku” due to a suddenly discovered heart disease. In a new place, he will have the opportunity to understand his own life and find a cure for the abyss of dreams into which his illness plunged him.
