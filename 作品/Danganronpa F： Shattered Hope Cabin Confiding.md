## 简介（VNDB 原文）

Similar to that of the "Salmon Mode" and "Love Suite" events of Danganronpa V3, Cabin Confiding is a spin-off game from Danganronpa F: Shattered Hope. It features the characters in Danganronpa F: Shattered Hope talking about what they think of love. Unlike the Love Hotel events in V3, these events are less sexual and more of a heart-to-heart with the characters.
